Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is CommandCenterFinance?
A US-based maker of spreadsheet templates — finance "command centers" — for high-stakes money moments: running a small business, estimating quarterly taxes, comparing contractor bids, controlling renovation costs, reconciling medical bills, and coordinating care for a family member. Templates are sold as instant digital downloads on Etsy.
Where can I buy the templates?
In the CommandCenterFinance shop on Etsy. Each purchase is an instant digital download: an .xlsx spreadsheet plus a Quick-Start PDF guide. Nothing is shipped.
Do the spreadsheets work in Google Sheets?
Yes. Every template is built in Microsoft Excel and verified in Google Sheets and LibreOffice, with import instructions included. There are no macros, no add-ins, and no locked passwords — formulas are protected against accidental edits but always visible.
Are these subscriptions?
No. Every template is a one-time purchase. You own the file forever and can reuse it every year.
How are the formulas verified?
Before a template is listed, it passes a structural audit, a full recalculation checked against hand-derived expected values, an error-cell scan, and live mutation tests. Every template ships with realistic sample data so you can see exactly how it behaves before entering your own numbers.
Do the templates give tax, legal, or medical advice?
No. The templates are planning and organization tools. The quarterly tax estimator produces planning estimates, not tax advice; the medical bill tracker organizes bills and EOBs but is not billing or legal advice. For advice, consult a qualified professional.
