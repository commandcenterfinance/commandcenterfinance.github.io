Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who are these for?
Project managers, project engineers, and estimators at general contractors. The people who run the paperwork side of a job: submittals, RFIs, punch, closeout, and buyout. These are A-side tools. They do not do labor burden, cost to complete, or WIP. That work belongs in your accounting system, not here.
Where do I buy them, and what do I get?
On Gumroad, instant download. You get a blank workbook, a sample workbook filled out on a realistic job, and a quick-start PDF. Nothing ships and there is no subscription. You buy the file once and it is yours.
Do they run in Excel, Google Sheets, and LibreOffice?
Yes, all three. Built in Excel, checked in Google Sheets and LibreOffice. No macros, no add-ins, no password on the formulas. They are protected so you do not wipe one out by accident, but you can open any tab and see exactly how a number is built.
Are there AIA forms in here, or set contract terms?
No. There is no AIA form and no CSI spec list. Review periods, response times, retainage, and every other contract term come from your contract, and you enter them. The workbook ships with no default values and assumes no standard, because every job's terms are different.
Is this legal or contract advice?
No. These track and organize. They are not your contract, your General Conditions, or your attorney. Before you rely on a date or a number the workbook gives you, check it against the contract.
